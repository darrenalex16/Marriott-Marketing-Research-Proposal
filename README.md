# Marriott International: Marketing Research Proposal

### Market Research · Research Design · Customer Behaviour · Statistical Methods

Developed an academic marketing research proposal exploring travel trends, guest satisfaction, loyalty programmes, and sustainability within the hospitality industry, using Marriott International as the case company.

**Author:** Darren Alexander  
**Course:** Statistical Methods for Market Research (ST3188)  
**Institution:** University of London, SIM Global Education

## Project Objectives

- Investigate emerging travel trends and the relationship between economic factors and travel behaviour.
- Assess guest satisfaction and preferences for personalised services and technology.
- Explore how loyalty programmes and sustainability initiatives relate to customer loyalty.

## Research Design

- Structured the proposal around **3 research aims and 6 research objectives**.
- Designed an online survey approach targeting **5,000 Marriott customers and 1,000 non-Marriott customers**.
- Proposed **10 focus groups** covering Marriott customers, competitor customers, and travellers using alternative accommodation.
- Developed questionnaires covering demographics, travel preferences, satisfaction, technology, and loyalty.

## Proposed Analytical Methods

| Method | Intended Application |
|---|---|
| Multiple linear regression | Explore relationships involving economic factors, travel behaviour, and customer loyalty |
| Independent-samples t-test | Compare satisfaction between corporate and leisure customers |
| Factor analysis | Explore underlying dimensions of preferences for services and technology |
| Ward’s cluster analysis | Explore groupings related to sustainability initiatives |
